The `Jurnal` strip at the bottom of the window (9.1-boʻlim). `heading` title, `surface-200` fill, `radius-lg`, a thin `border` rule instead of a shadow — it's a docked panel, not a floating one. Lines are `code` (mono) so timestamps and Uzbek text share a baseline without the numbers drifting: a leading `[SS:DD:SS]` timestamp in `ink-tertiary`, the message in `ink-secondary`, except an `XATO:` line, which takes `danger` for the whole line so a failure doesn't have to be found by reading every row. New lines append at the bottom and auto-scroll unless the person has scrolled up, exactly like a terminal.

`space-3` padding, `space-1` between lines, no line-wrapping — a long path or filename truncates with an ellipsis rather than pushing the panel taller; the full line is available on hover as a tooltip or on click to copy.
