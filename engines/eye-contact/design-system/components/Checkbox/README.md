A single labelled box, for the one binary setting the toolbar exposes today: `HEVC belgisi` (`--codec hevc` vs. the h264 default, 9.2-boʻlim). 16px square, `radius-sm`, `border-strong` outline and `surface-200` fill at rest; checked swaps to `accent` fill with an `on-accent` check mark, no intermediate colour. Label sits `space-2` to the right in `body`, and the whole row — box and label — is the click target, not the box alone. Keyboard focus is the `focus-ring` shadow around the box only. Disabled (e.g. `HEVC` while `--lossless` is set, since FFV1 ignores the codec choice) drops to `surface-400`/`ink-tertiary` for both box and label.

Reach for this, not a switch: everything here is a project setting read back from `gui_settings.json` at launch, not a live toggle with an immediate side effect — a checkbox is the honest shape for that.
